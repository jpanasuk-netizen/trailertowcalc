Best Hitch for a Ford F-250

Quick answer: The best hitch for an F-250 has to match its factory 2.5-inch receiver, which Ford's 2026 Super Duty towing guide rates at 22,000 lb with 2,200 lb of tongue load, weight carrying or weight distributing, and 18,200 lb on the Tremor. Ford says conventional tongue load should be 10% of loaded trailer weight.

Your trailer on an F-250 SRW

Pick an engine row from Ford's conventional towing table and enter the loaded trailer weight. The checker tests it against the row and the receiver ratings, and prints Ford's 10% tongue target. For a kingpin or ball-in-bed trailer, start with fifth wheel hitches instead.

Which hitch for which trailer

The receiver is rated above every F-250 conventional row except the 22,000 lb ones, which it matches, so on most trucks the engine row is the limit, not the receiver. Ford says conventional tongue load should be 10% of loaded trailer weight (15% for 5th-wheel and gooseneck); check yours with the tongue weight guide. Towing around 15,000 lb?
