Why the "lowest number" rule matters

A truck is a system of ratings, and towing capacity marketing quotes only the biggest one. The tow rating is what the drivetrain can pull. The GCWR caps truck + trailer together. The payload rating caps everything sitting on the truck — including the trailer's tongue weight. The hitch class caps what the receiver itself can hold. Your real capacity is the smallest of the four after the load is actually applied. Half-ton trucks towing big campers almost always hit the payload limit first, at tongue weights of 800–1,000 lb — long before the tow rating is close.
