Best hitch for Subaru Forester

Quick answer: Subaru rates most 2026 Forester trims to tow 1,500 lbs and the Forester Wilderness to 3,500 lbs, thanks to an uprated transmission cooler. Subaru says not to use any weight-distributing hitch. CURT's Class 1 is rated to 2,000 lbs and Class 2 to 3,500 lbs, so a Class 2 hitch covers the Wilderness.
