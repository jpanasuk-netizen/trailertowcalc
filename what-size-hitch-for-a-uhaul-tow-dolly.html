What size hitch for a U-Haul tow dolly?

Quick answer: U-Haul lists its tow dolly at 740 lbs empty with a 4,080 lb max load, about 4,820 lbs fully loaded. U-Haul requires a 2 in. ball rated for at least 3,500 lbs, a ball height of 25 in. or less, and 55 mph max. That is past CURT's 3,500 lb Class 2 and inside Class 3, rated to 8,000 lbs.

Your car on the dolly

Dolly plus car is 740 lbs plus your car, capped at the 4,080 lb max load. The full 4,820 is 740 + 4,080.

Why Class 3

A loaded dolly at about 4,820 lbs is past Class 2's 3,500 lb rating, and Class 3 runs to 8,000 lbs. A light car can bring the total under 3,500 lbs, but U-Haul still asks for a ball rated at least 3,500 lbs. The 5,000 lb trailer page lands in the same class. Carrying the whole car instead? The U-Haul car trailer is 7,500 lbs loaded.
