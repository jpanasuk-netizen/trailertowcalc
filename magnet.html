Payload

The sticker on the door is the truck. The billboard is a rumor.

Tow rating is what the drivetrain can pull. Payload is what the truck can hold, including tongue weight, people, and gear. The brochure number is not the limit by itself.

Your real limit is the smallest of tow rating, GCWR, payload, and hitch class. Read them on the door-jamb sticker and in the owner's manual, then enter them in the tow calculator. CURT's tongue-weight range is 10–15% of loaded trailer weight. NHTSA says to look up GVWR, the maximum trailer weight, GCWR, and GAWR, and to use the loaded weight rather than the dry weight.

Before you leave. Read the door-jamb sticker and the owner's manual. Weigh the loaded trailer. Enter those figures in the calculator. This card does not give a class-average payload, a tow rating, or a tongue percentage other than CURT's 10–15% band.
