Best Hitch for a Ford F-150

Quick answer: The best hitch for an F-150 depends on trailer weight. Ford's 2026 towing guide rates the factory 2-inch receiver at 5,000 lb and 500 lb tongue load as weight carrying, or up to 13,500 lb and 1,400 lb with a weight-distribution hitch. That 13,500 lb needs the 3.5L EcoBoost V6, Tow/Haul Package and Max Tow Axle.

Which hitch for which trailer

Up to 5,000 lb, the factory receiver works weight carrying, with up to 500 lb on the tongue. Above 5,000 lb, Ford requires a weight-distribution system, and the receiver's weight-distributing rating goes up to 13,500 lb and 1,400 lb of tongue, but only as far as your engine's row allows. Ford's tongue load target is 10% of loaded trailer weight; check yours with the tongue weight guide. Ball and class ratings are in the hitch class and ball ratings data.
