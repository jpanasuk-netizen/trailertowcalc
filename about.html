About & how this site is built

Who

Built and owned by Jeremy Panasuk, New Auburn, Wisconsin. Contact: jpanasuk@gmail.com. Jeremy is a data/software person, not a licensed automotive or towing professional; always follow the vehicle manufacturer's ratings. These are planning tools, not professional advice.

How

The calculators use published rules of thumb from the sources listed on each page. Page text is drafted with AI agents (Cursor and local models) from those sources. Formulas are checked against the cited sources. If you spot an error, email jpanasuk@gmail.com and it gets fixed.

Why

The site earns money from Amazon affiliate links. That doesn't change the math.
