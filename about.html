About & how this site is built

Who

Built and owned by Jeremy Panasuk, New Auburn, Wisconsin. Contact: jpanasuk@gmail.com. Jeremy is a data/software person, not a licensed automotive or towing professional; always follow the vehicle manufacturer's ratings. These are planning tools, not professional advice.

How

The calculators use published rules of thumb from the sources listed on each page. Page text is drafted with AI agents (Cursor and local models) from those sources. Formulas are checked against the cited sources. If you spot an error, email jpanasuk@gmail.com and it gets fixed.

Why

The site earns money from Amazon affiliate links. That doesn't change the math.

How the calculators, sources, and affiliate links are handled is on the methodology page.

Corrections

Report a wrong figure to jpanasuk@gmail.com with the page and the source that shows the right number. When the source confirms it, the page is fixed and its Updated date changes. Towing ratings change by model year; tell me what's out of date. This page was updated November 24, 2026.

What I've built

The same person builds these free calculators, with the same sourcing rules:

  • GeneratorSizer: generator size, fuel and transfer-switch calculators
  • SolarSizer: off-grid solar array, battery bank and charge controller sizing
  • BatteryBankSizer: battery bank size and runtime
  • WeldingCalc: welding amps by metal thickness and duty cycle
  • HVACLoadCalc: air conditioner and heat pump BTU sizing
  • ChickenCoopCalc: chicken coop and run space
  • AquariumStocking: aquarium stocking, heater and filter size
