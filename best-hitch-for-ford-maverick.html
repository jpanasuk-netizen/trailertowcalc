Best hitch for Ford Maverick

Quick answer: Ford rates the 2026 Maverick to tow 2,000 lbs as standard. The optional 4K Tow Package raises that to 4,000 lbs on AWD models and adds a Class III 2 in. receiver, a trailer brake controller and an upgraded cooling fan. CURT's Class 2 tops out at 3,500 lbs, so 4,000 lbs needs Class 3.

The same guide lists a separate 2K Trailer Hitch Receiver with a 4-pin connector, and that option is not available with the 4K package.

A trailer over 2,000 lbs is past the standard rating, so the 4K package is the published way to the 4,000 lb figure. The Class III receiver comes with that package. It is not a separate hitch you add on top of a truck that is already at 4,000 lbs. Tremor and Lobo stay at 2,000 lbs on the tech-specs sheet, and the order guide does not list 53Q for them.

Class 2 stops at 3,500 lbs, so a trailer at the Maverick's 4,000 lb package rating is a Class 3 weight. That matches the Class III 2 in. receiver in the 4K package. A trailer at the standard 2,000 lb rating is at the top of Class 1.
