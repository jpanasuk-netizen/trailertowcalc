Trailer hitch class and hitch ball weight ratings

CURT rates Class 1 hitches up to 2,000 lbs gross trailer weight and 200 lbs tongue weight, Class 3 up to 8,000 and 800, and Class 4 up to 10,000 and 1,000. CURT's 2-inch balls are rated 3,500 to 12,000 lbs. Your rating is capped by the lowest-rated part, including the vehicle.

Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Built by Jeremy Panasuk.

Ratings vary by part number, and your towing limit is capped by the lowest-rated part in the setup, including the vehicle, as published by CURT. WD means with a weight-distribution hitch. "--" means CURT lists no weight-distribution rating for that class.
