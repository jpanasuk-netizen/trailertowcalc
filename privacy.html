Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

TrailerTowCalc (trailertowcalc.com) is a free calculator site owned by Jeremy Panasuk, New Auburn, Wisconsin. It is designed to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

The calculators run entirely in your browser. The values you enter (tow rating, GCWR, payload, trailer weight, tongue weight, and the other fields on the page) are not transmitted to or stored on our servers — they exist only in your browser session. We do not require accounts and do not collect names, emails, or personal profiles through the calculators.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links to Amazon.com through the Amazon Associates program. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Amazon links on this site are affiliate links; they do not change the math.

Analytics & cookies

We do not run our own analytics, and we do not load Google AdSense or any other advertising script. We do not set tracking cookies.

On the homepage, assets/monetization.js builds Amazon search links in your browser. If you dismiss the Amazon bar, that script stores a flag in sessionStorage (amzStickyDismissed) so the bar stays closed for the rest of the browser session. That flag stays on your device and is not sent to us. assets/app.js only runs the calculators. The homepage and the topic map include a link to the printable pocket card and a link to a TikTok profile in the page HTML. Those are ordinary links. This site does not embed TikTok, Amazon, or Google scripts.

If you follow a link to Amazon or TikTok, that site may set its own cookies under its own policy. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
