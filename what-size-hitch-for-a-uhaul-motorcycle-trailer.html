What size hitch for a U-Haul motorcycle trailer?

Quick answer: U-Haul lists its motorcycle trailer at 870 lbs empty with a 1,530 lb max load, or 2,400 lbs combined, on a 1-7/8 or 2 in. ball. That is over CURT's 2,000 lb Class 1 rating and inside Class 2, rated to 3,500 lbs. U-Haul says your vehicle's curb weight must exceed the loaded trailer.

Why Class 2

A loaded motorcycle trailer is 870 + 1,530 = 2,400 lbs. Class 1 hitches stop at 2,000 lbs, so a full load is past them. Class 2 is rated to 3,500 lbs and covers it with room left over. The 3,000 lb trailer page lands in the same class.

Either ball size works here, as long as it is rated for at least 2,000 lbs. If you already have a 2 in. ball from a utility trailer, it fits. No brakes are listed, and U-Haul's page says the vehicle's curb weight must exceed the loaded trailer, so plan on at least 2,400 lbs of tow vehicle with a full load. CURT puts tongue weight at about 10 to 15% of gross trailer weight; see the tongue weight guide.

The U-Haul 5x8 cargo trailer is a close match at 2,700 lbs loaded. Hauling a car instead? See the U-Haul car trailer page.
