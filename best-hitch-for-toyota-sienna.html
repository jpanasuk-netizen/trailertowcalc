Best hitch for Toyota Sienna

Built by Jeremy Panasuk. Published November 17, 2026.

Quick answer: Toyota lists up to 3,500 lb of towing capacity for the 2026 Sienna, and its Woodland Edition adds a tow hitch with that capability. CURT rates a Class 2 hitch up to 3,500 lb gross trailer weight and 350 lb tongue weight, which exactly matches that, and says no hitch ever increases the maximum weight a vehicle can tow.
