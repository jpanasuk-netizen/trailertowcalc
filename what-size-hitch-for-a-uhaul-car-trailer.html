What size hitch for a U-Haul car trailer?

Quick answer: U-Haul lists its auto transport trailer at 2,210 lbs empty with a 5,290 lb max load and a 7,500 lb gross weight, with a surge brake and a required 2 in. ball. That is past CURT's 3,500 lb Class 2 rating and inside Class 3, rated to 8,000 lbs. Your vehicle's curb weight must exceed the loaded trailer.

Why Class 3 and not Class 2

The number that sets the hitch class is the loaded trailer, not the car you are hauling. 2,210 lbs of trailer plus a 5,290 lb max load is 7,500 lbs. Class 2 stops at 3,500 lbs, so even a light car on this trailer can push past it. Class 3 runs to 8,000 lbs and covers the full 7,500 lb gross. The same rating sits on the 8,000 lb trailer page.

The surge brake is built in, so there is no brake controller to wire. See does my trailer need brakes? for how surge brakes work. U-Haul's rental page also says the curb weight of the vehicle must exceed the weight of the loaded trailer, and the calculator above checks that. CURT puts tongue weight at about 10 to 15% of gross trailer weight; see the tongue weight guide.
