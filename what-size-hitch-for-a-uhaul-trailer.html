What size hitch do I need for a U-Haul trailer?

Quick answer: U-Haul's rentals fall into two CURT hitch classes. The motorcycle trailer (2,400 lbs loaded) and the 5x8 cargo trailer (2,700 lbs) fit Class 2, rated to 3,500 lbs. The 6x12 cargo trailer (4,400 lbs), the tow dolly (about 4,820 lbs) and the auto transport (7,500 lbs) need Class 3, rated to 8,000 lbs.

Ball size and vehicle weight

Ball size varies by trailer. The motorcycle and 5x8 trailers take a 1-7/8 or 2 in. ball rated at least 2,000 lbs. The auto transport needs a 2 in. ball, and the tow dolly a 2 in. ball rated at least 3,500 lbs. U-Haul also checks your vehicle: its trailer user instructions tie the loaded trailer to your vehicle's curb weight.
