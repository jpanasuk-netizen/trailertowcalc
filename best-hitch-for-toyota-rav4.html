Best hitch for Toyota RAV4

Built by Jeremy Panasuk. Published November 17, 2026.

Quick answer: Toyota's newsroom lists the 2026 RAV4 at 1,750 lb of towing capacity on the LE grade and 3,500 lb on the AWD XLE Premium and Woodland. CURT rates a Class 2 hitch up to 3,500 lb with 350 lb of tongue weight, and says no hitch raises what a vehicle can tow.

XLE Premium3,500 lbs“3,500 lbs. Towing Capacity AWD”
Woodland3,500 lbs“3,500 lbs. Towing Capacity”, with a 2-in. rear tow hitch receiver with wiring harness

Source: Toyota Newsroom, “The Next Adventure Begins: 2026 RAV4 Arrives this Winter”, read November 17, 2026. The 2026 RAV4 brochure also says “up to 3500 lbs. of towing capacity”, in its Woodland section.

The LE row in Toyota's release is labeled AWD. If you have a front-drive LE, the release does not print a figure for it; check the owner's manual or a Toyota dealer before towing.

Source: CURT, types of trailer hitches, read November 17, 2026. CURT says not all hitches are rated at the same capacity and that no hitch ever increases the maximum weight a vehicle can tow.

A trailer up to 1,750 lbs is inside Class 1, which CURT rates to 2,000 lbs. A 3,500 lb trailer on an XLE Premium AWD or Woodland is at the top of Class 2. The Woodland's factory receiver is 2 in., the receiver size CURT lists for Class 3; the RAV4's 3,500 lb rating still applies, because no hitch raises it.
